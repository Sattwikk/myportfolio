About Me

I am a Computer Science graduate student at Georgia State University with a strong interest in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and software development.

My academic journey has allowed me to explore topics ranging from computer networks and programming to intelligent systems and mobile application development. I enjoy understanding how technology works and applying that knowledge to practical problems.

My research interests include computer vision, deep learning, reinforcement learning, spiking neural networks, and graph-based modeling. I am particularly interested in developing intelligent systems that can address real-world challenges.

Outside of coursework and research, I enjoy building software projects and learning new technologies. I believe that consistent learning, experimentation, and problem solving are essential parts of becoming a better software engineer.
